Client Success Story

From outdated and difficult to manage to a polished, lead-generating website.

Juan A1 Custom Countertops LLC came to us with a clear problem: their website didn’t reflect the quality of their craftsmanship and couldn’t be updated easily.

There was no real brand identity, the site was not mobile responsive, it was slow, and their previous marketer was unreachable for edits. They needed a more professional online presence that could support growth and help them reach more clients.

We rebuilt the website from the ground up, created a custom logo to give their company stronger identity, added a custom domain with the LLC, improved their sales process with a quote request form, and expanded their online capabilities with an eCommerce store, invoicing support, business email, and professionally branded business cards.

“Solid Site did a great job on rebuilding my old website and making us look more professional. They also provided all the training for how to use our new software.”

— Juan A1 Custom Countertops LLC
